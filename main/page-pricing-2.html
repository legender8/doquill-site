Questions enterprises ask before switching to DoQuill

  • What is included in every plan?

    Every DoQuill plan includes all core platform modules — Proposal, Sales Invoice, Purchase, Product & Service, Media Library, Messenger, AI Agent, Accounting, HRM, Inventory, CRM, Sales, POS, Project, MailBox, Make, Stripe, PayPal, Webhook, Workflow Automation and API Docs Generator. No modules are locked behind higher tiers.

  • What is the difference between pre-package and usage subscription?

    Pre-package plans are priced per user per month with a minimum number of users — ideal for teams of 5 or more. Usage subscription is for teams under 5 users who pay $25 per user and add modules at $9/mo and industry verticals at $19/mo as needed.

  • What are industry vertical systems?

    Industry verticals are complete operational management systems built for specific sectors — Hospital Management, Construction, Agriculture, Freight and 46+ more. Each vertical includes all the sub-modules, workflows and reports for that industry. Starter includes 1, Growth includes 3, Enterprise includes 5.

  • Can I add more industry verticals?

    Yes. Additional verticals are available on any plan at $19/mo each. Activation covers your entire workspace — all users, no per-seat charge.

  • Is there a free trial?

    Yes. All pre-package plans include a 7-day free trial. No credit card required. You get access to all core modules and your included industry vertical from day one.

  • Do you offer yearly billing discounts?

    Yes. All plans — pre-package and usage subscription — receive 20% off when billed yearly. This applies to the base plan, additional modules and additional industry verticals.

  • What is the Custom Development service?

    DoQuill offers two bespoke automation services built on top of your subscription. Service 1 ($350 one-time) connects your ERP to WhatsApp — instant notifications for invoices, POs and quotes. Service 2 ($750 one-time) adds AI-powered email document capture — supplier invoices are read, extracted and posted to your ERP automatically.

  • How quickly can we go live?

    Most teams are fully operational within 3 to 5 business days. No implementation consultants required. Onboarding documentation and support are included in every plan.
