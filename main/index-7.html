Questions enterprises ask before switching to DoQuill

  • What ERP modules are included in every plan?

    Every DoQuill plan includes all 13 core platform modules — Sales, Purchase, Quotation, Product & Service, Accounting, Double Entry, Inventory, HRM, Document, Mail, Workflow Automation, API & Webhook and AI Agent. No modules are locked behind higher tiers.

  • What are industry vertical systems?

    DoQuill has 96 full industry management systems — from Hospital Management to Construction, Agriculture to Freight. Each is a complete operational system built specifically for that industry. Not a dashboard — a full sub-system with its own data model, workflows and reporting.

  • How does the addon marketplace work?

    All 300+ platform addons are available on every plan via a one-time activation fee. No recurring charge. Once activated it covers your entire workspace — all users included.

  • Can DoQuill integrate with tools we already use?

    Yes. DoQuill integrates with Shopify, Xero, Salesforce, Google Drive, Slack, Twilio, WhatsApp Business and 58 payment gateways including Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe and mobile money providers across Africa.

  • What is the WhatsApp automation?

    DoQuill offers a custom AI automation service that connects your ERP to WhatsApp. When an invoice is raised, a PO approved or a document received — your team gets an instant WhatsApp notification automatically. Available as a one-time installation service from $350.

  • How quickly can we go live?

    Most businesses are operational within days. Your industry vertical arrives pre-configured for your sector. No lengthy implementation. No consultants required. Our team handles setup and your team is running from day one.

  • Is DoQuill available in our language?

    DoQuill supports 15+ languages including English, French, Spanish, German, Arabic, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and more — with full RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew.

  • What does the per-user pricing mean exactly?

    You pay for the number of active users in your organisation each month. All 13 core modules, AI Agent, Workflow Automation and API access are included in every plan regardless of user count. As your team grows your plan scales with you.
