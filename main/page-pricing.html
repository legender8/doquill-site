Frequently asked questions

  • Is there a minimum user requirement?

    Yes. The Starter plan requires a minimum of 3 users, Growth requires a minimum of 10 users, and Enterprise requires a minimum of 25 users. This ensures your team can fully leverage the platform’s collaboration and workflow capabilities from day one.

  • Can I add more users at any time?

    Yes. You can add users at any point during your subscription. Additional seats are prorated to your current billing cycle so you only pay for what you use. There is no ceiling on the number of users across all plans.

  • Which payment methods do you accept?

    DoQuill accepts major credit and debit cards, bank transfers, and African payment gateways including M-Pesa, Flutterwave, and Paystack — ensuring frictionless billing for enterprises across Africa and emerging markets.

  • What is included in the free trial?

    The free trial gives you full access to all features on your selected plan for 14 days. No credit card is required. At the end of the trial you can choose to activate your subscription or your data will be retained for 30 days should you decide to return.

  • How does the Enterprise plan differ from Growth?

    Enterprise adds the full Predictive Intelligence Layer, Conversational Action Agent, Automated 3-Way Matching, multi-entity consolidation, SSO, custom user roles, and an SLA-backed uptime guarantee. For mining and heavy industry operations, bespoke pricing and on-premise deployment options are available — contact our sales team to discuss your requirements.

  • Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

    Yes. Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Upgrading mid-cycle is prorated immediately so your team gains access to additional features without delay. Downgrading follows the same cycle boundary to avoid disruption to active workflows.
