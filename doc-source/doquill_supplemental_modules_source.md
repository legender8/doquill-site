## INDUSTRY VERTICALS

### 1. Consignment Management System
**What it is:** A full end-to-end system for managing consigned goods between vendors, consignors, and customers.
**Core Features:**
- Product Management — Manage consignment product catalog with pricing, quantity, SKU, category, weight, and tax fields; assign multiple products to a single consignment
- Consignment Management — Create and track consignment records with commission rates, auto-calculated subtotals/totals, and vendor assignments
- Purchase Orders — Create POs linked to consignments with auto-fetched product details; convert POs into finalized purchases with full traceability
- Sales Orders — Generate sale orders tied to consignments; auto-fill item/price data; convert into invoices for commission payout
- Shipping & Delivery Management — Attach shipping details (carrier, tracking number, status, estimated dates) to each consignment
- Returns Management — Record product returns by sales order/consignment with reason, quantity, date, and return status
- Quality Control — Log product inspections (date, inspector, status, remarks) to ensure only approved goods move forward
- Compliance & Contract Management — Manage supply and purchase contracts with start/end dates, involved parties, and terms & conditions
**Best for:** Retail stores, art galleries, second-hand shops, bookstores, furniture showrooms
