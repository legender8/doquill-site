Terms of use

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

DoQuill uses cookies on doquill.io and app.doquill.io for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Authentication cookies — Keep you logged in to your account securely.
  • Preference cookies — Remember your settings such as language and dark/light mode.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.
  • Marketing cookies — Used to show relevant advertisements when you visit other websites.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies including:

  • Google Analytics — for website usage analysis

  • Meta Pixel — for advertising and retargeting
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — for B2B advertising
  • Tawk.to — for live chat functionality

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Through your browser settings — most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies
  • Through our cookie consent banner when you first visit our site
  • By opting out of specific third-party cookies through their respective opt-out tools

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platform.

5. Cookie Retention

Different cookies are retained for different periods:

  • Session cookies — deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies — remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted
  • Authentication cookies — typically retained for 30 days

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of our platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies please contact us:

  • Email: contact@doquill.io
  • Website: www.doquill.io
